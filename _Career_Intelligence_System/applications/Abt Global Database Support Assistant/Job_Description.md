## Purpose

Under close supervision of the MEL Database Manager, support the integrity, accuracy and completeness of IRS campaign data: daily database support, data entry, cleaning and synchronisation between field teams and central databases, particularly DHIS2.

## Key responsibilities

### Data quality assurance and verification
- Run daily duplicate reports in DHIS2, analyse outputs, and share lists with field teams for resolution.
- Input field feedback to update and resolve duplicate entries.
- Identify and update missing data fields, including GPS coordinates, from verified field reports.
- Run outlier checks and correct data in line with MEL guidance and feedback protocols.
- Conduct regular and extensive data verification and cleaning.
- Maintain daily tracking of data submissions per spray operator and cross-verify team leader reports.
- Escalate inconsistencies or gaps to the MEL Assistant and Assistant MEL Database Manager.

### Data entry and management
- Enter collected data from paper into the central database, ensuring completeness.
- Support updating and reconciliation of support staff and season staff.
- Generate and print requested reports for MEL staff or field supervisors.
- File complete data forms and reports in line with data protection and project protocols.

## Requirements

- Bachelor's in Biostatistics, Statistics, Computer Science, IT, Data Science or a numeric discipline + 2 years relevant experience (1 year in IRS an advantage), OR Diploma + 2 years (1 year in IRS preferred).
- Proven data entry, cleaning and database management.
- Comfortable using and troubleshooting ODK/Kobo Collect.
- Familiarity and hands-on experience with DHIS2 required (academy.dhis2.org certification).
- Microsoft Office and standard database tools.
- Spreadsheets: formulas, filter and sort large datasets.
- Interpersonal, communication and analytical skills; independent under pressure; integrity; six days a week.

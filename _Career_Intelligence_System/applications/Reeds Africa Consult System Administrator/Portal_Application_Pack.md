- **AWS is familiarity, not operations.** Their line is "AWS/Azure/O365" and you satisfy it through Azure and O365. If AWS comes up: you have worked with AWS and Docker at a familiarity level and your depth is Azure. Do not claim AWS production administration.

## Ask the recruiter these

Agency calls are two-way and they expect questions. Ask:

1. **Who is the client, and what industry?** You cannot judge the role without it.
2. **How big is the environment** — users, sites, servers, and is it on-premise, cloud or hybrid?
3. **Is there an existing ICT team, and would I be mentoring juniors** as the JD suggests?
4. **Is there on-call or after-hours rotation?**
5. **What is the client's budgeted salary band?**

# Job Description — Customer Support Specialist, Influx

*Captured 2026-07-15 from user-provided posting. Confirm apply link and exact title on live listing.*

## Company

Influx is a profitable, growing startup providing on-demand 24/7 customer support for leading brands and technology companies. Global team of 1,000+ members.

## Role summary

Full-time, **work-from-home** customer support role (44 hours/week), remote from **anywhere in Kenya**. Email, live chat, and phone support with basic technical troubleshooting using guided processes and knowledge base.

## What you'll do

- Deliver exceptional customer support through email, live chat, and phone.
- Resolve enquiries using established support processes and knowledge base articles.
- Troubleshoot common technical issues: login/password, account access, basic product setup, application or website errors, connectivity/user-facing issues using guided steps.
- Explain solutions clearly in a professional, customer-friendly manner.
- Document interactions and categorize support tickets accurately.
- Escalate complex or non-standard issues to specialized teams.
- Identify recurring issues and feedback to improve processes and documentation.
- Collaborate to continuously improve customer experience.

## Who you are

- Excellent English (spoken and written).
- **At least 1 year** in Customer Service, Customer Support, Helpdesk, Service Desk, or similar customer-facing roles.
- Comfortable with basic technical troubleshooting via structured guides and workflows.
- Familiar with web applications, mobile apps, online platforms.
- Quick to learn new software; navigate multiple systems.
- Problem-solving via established troubleshooting procedures.
- Calm, patient, empathetic.
- Strong attention to detail and documentation.
- Comfortable escalating outside documented procedures.
- **Advantage:** CRM/helpdesk (Zendesk, Intercom, Freshdesk, Salesforce, HubSpot, or similar).
- **Preferred:** user accounts, browsers, passwords, internet connectivity, software configuration.

## Ideal background titles

Customer Support Representative, Customer Service Agent, Helpdesk Agent, SaaS Customer Support, E-commerce Customer Support, BPO Customer Support, Call Center Customer Service.

100% remote, collaborative global environment, international clients, growth opportunities.

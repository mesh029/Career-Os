# BruntWork recording templates (voice, video, technical check)

Reference for every BruntWork portal application. Copy into the pack's `Portal_Application_Pack.md` and fill the brackets for the role.
Facts must match `resumes/00_Master_Resume.md`.

## 1. Voice recording — BruntWork template (under 1 minute)

Three lines. Keep the bold phrases word for word; they are BruntWork's prompt.

>
> **I take pride in** [two or three strengths the role asks for, said plainly].
>
> **I look forward to showing how I can be of value to your company.** I am in Kenya, and I am available [days and hours in the client's time zone].

### Delivery

- Sit up, smile slightly (it changes the voice), speak a little slower than normal.
- Glance at the lines; do not read like a script.
- Quiet room, door closed, wired headset.
- Last take wins. Re-record if you stumble.
- No colons in spoken scripts. Use full stops or commas so the lines read naturally aloud.
- If short on time, drop the hours sentence; never drop "I look forward to showing how I can be of value to your company."

## 3. Technical check

| Item | Target |
|---|---|
| Internet | Stable broadband, wired if possible. speedtest.net on the work machine, screenshot it. |
| Backup internet | Phone hotspot or second line, tested |
| Computer | Windows 10/11, 8 GB RAM minimum (16 GB better), recent i5 or equal |
| Headset | Wired, noise-cancelling mic, tested in Sound settings |
| Webcam | Working, face lit, plain background |
| Power | Know the fallback (UPS, power bank for the router). Do not claim one you do not have. |
| Quiet room | Door closed, no TV, no echo |

Run the check at the hour you would actually work (for Australian or US clients that is often night in Kenya).

## 5. Reusable assessment answer patterns

- **Device offline:** check the platform (last check-in, one device or many) → remote reset → guide the client (power, cable, Wi-Fi, moved?) → record it in the ticket → escalate with a clean summary → flag patterns. Proof: Meraki dashboard and remote reboot.
- **SLA about to breach, waiting on another team:** chase directly with ticket, impact and SLA time → update the client before the deadline → escalate through the agreed path. "Clients forgive delays; they don't forgive silence."
- **Upset client:** let them finish → acknowledge the impact → confirm details → say what you'll do and when you'll update → keep the promise.
- **Competing priorities:** impact and SLA first → closest to breach → quick wins between → timebox, document, escalate.
- **Recurring issues:** consistent tags, weekly review, group and send the pattern, update the guide. Proof: SQL checks cut reporting time ~40%.
- **Non-technical explanation:** the shared-folder access story (access changed after a colleague left; restored, five-line guide).
- **Where did you hear about BruntWork:** say where you actually saw it (LinkedIn, BruntWork job board, a friend). Use the same answer every time.
- **Rate:** set from the application. Only enter a number if forced, and do not lowball.

## 6. Never say

- "I have not used", "I haven't", "no experience with". Frame the tool by the job it does and what you already run.
- Any daily ticket count. About 20 tickets a week; lead with 120+ users instead.
- "HIPAA certified", "Intune expert", or any tool or certification not in the master CV.
- Upwork or named freelance clients.
- Pay, "I need this job", or complaints about PATH.
- BruntWork never asks for money. Never pay for training or equipment.

## Gap

**No SAP, Syspro, Navision, Dynamics 365 or Sage.** The cover letter says so in one sentence and pivots to the shared disciplines. Do not tick or type any of these as experience. If asked in interview:

> "I haven't administered SAP or Dynamics. What I have done for three years is support business-critical systems end to end: users, roles, data quality, vendors, licences, backups and SLAs. The screens are different; the job is the same. I'd start with your super users and the support documentation."

Quick wins before an interview (1 to 2 evenings): SAP Learning Hub free "SAP S/4HANA overview" or Microsoft Learn "Dynamics 365 fundamentals (MB-910) learning path". Then you can say "I've started", truthfully.

## Likely interview questions

1. A user in Finance cannot post invoices since this morning. Walk through it. (Scope: one user or many; recent role or password change; check authorisation; reproduce; check system health; escalate to the consultant with screenshots and steps.)
2. Risk says a user can both create and approve purchase orders. What do you do? (Confirm, raise with the process owner, remove the conflicting role, document, add to the periodic review.)
3. How do you keep a vendor to its SLA? (Log every ticket with timestamps, monthly review against contract, escalate breaches in writing.)
4. How would you plan an ERP update with minimal disruption? (Test in a sandbox, sign-off from key users, backup, off-hours window, rollback plan, communicate before and after.)

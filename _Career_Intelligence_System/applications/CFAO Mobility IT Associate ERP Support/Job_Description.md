# IT Associate, ERP Support

**Employer:** CFAO Mobility Kenya Limited (CFAO Group; Toyota, Yamaha, Volkswagen, Suzuki, Mercedes-Benz, Hino, Hyundai light trucks, Sinotruk, Winpart, AUTOFAST)
**Reports to:** IT Business Applications Administrator
**Location:** Head Office, Nairobi
**Deadline:** 7 October 2026, 5:00 PM
**How to apply:** web form on the CFAO Mobility careers page (one PDF CV upload). Only shortlisted candidates contacted.

## Purpose

Keep business systems available and optimally used, roles correctly assigned, and service desk tickets resolved, while giving first-line technical support.

## Duties

- **ERP support and SLA:** Level 1 support and training for users of SAP, Syspro, TALO, Navision, MS Dynamics 365, Synertrade, SAGE, Fleetwave, Annata and others; monitor and optimise performance; coordinate super users across the group and act as the link to the consulting firms; maintain SLAs; periodic satisfaction meetings with business users.
- **Integration, configuration, customisation:** integrate the ERP with other applications.
- **Security and compliance:** access controls, encryption, compliance; review user authorisations with Risk for segregation of duties.
- **Data and change management:** data integrity, imports, cleansing; testing, validation and QA of changes.
- **Backup and recovery:** backup and DR plans.
- **Upgrades and updates:** plan and execute with minimal disruption.
- **Licensing, payments, documentation:** pay recurrent bills on time; manage licensing for all business systems; maintain configuration, procedure and policy documentation.
- **Vendor management:** work with ERP vendors; negotiate contracts and SLAs; manage outsourced resources to contract SLA.
- **Training:** sessions and user guides.
- **Project management:** lead or contribute to upgrades, migrations, implementations; negotiate and follow up contract sign-off.
- **Asset management:** inventory and life cycle of business systems.
- **ITSM and general ICT support:** diagnostics and troubleshooting across systems, connectivity, security, backup, hardware.

## Requirements

- Bachelor's in Computer Science / IT or related.
- 2 years relevant experience.
- System applications architecture; problem solving and root cause analysis; ERP administration and functional knowledge; business process understanding.
- IT certification an added advantage.

## Form fields

Full name, phone, email, date of birth, nationality, highest qualification, institution, year graduated, years of relevant IT support experience, description of that experience (company and period), IT certifications, current salary, expected salary, notice period, three referees, CV upload (PDF), privacy consent.

## The work

Support internal users and external customers: hardware, software, networks, workplace systems, and digital customer platforms.

- Troubleshoot hardware, software, network, applications, access and digital platforms.
- Install and support desktops, peripherals, applications, meeting rooms, accounts and workplace equipment.
- Log, prioritise, document and escalate in line with service standards.
- Train users and customers on IT systems and Digital Customer Solutions.
- Asset inventory: deployment, lifecycle, SIM cards, mobile devices, replacements, audit-ready records.
- Technical input on evaluation, standardisation, replacement and procurement.
- Reports: incidents, SLA, volumes, recurring issues, adoption, feedback, improvements.
- Customer setup, shipping, deployment, onboarding and ongoing support for Digital Customer Solutions.
- Work with departments, customers, vendors, country and regional IT, and data-quality forums.

## What they ask

2–3 years in IT support, user support, technical service delivery, or digital customer solutions.

Working knowledge of troubleshooting, hardware, Microsoft 365, networking, ITSM, cybersecurity, SharePoint, Power Automate, Power Apps, basic data analysis, automation, integrations, and IT asset management.

Clear English with non-technical people. Customer focus. Compliance.

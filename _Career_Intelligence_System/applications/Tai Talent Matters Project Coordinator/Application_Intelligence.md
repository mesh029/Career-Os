## Likely interview questions

1. **A subcontractor is two days late and the client wants an update today.** Get the facts and a new date from the subcontractor, assess the impact on the milestone, tell the Head of Technical, then give the client an honest update with the recovery plan. Log it in the risk/issue log.
2. **How do you track a project with 20 sites?** One tracker: site, status, next action, owner, due date, blockers. Weekly status report from it. Flag red items early.
3. **What do you check before a site visit?** Access and permits, materials and tools on site, the right people present, power and safety, and a checklist for what "done" looks like.
4. **How do you handle a change request?** Log it, estimate the time and cost impact, route it for approval, and don't start the extra work until it is approved.
5. **Why project coordination?** "I already coordinate rollouts. I want to do it full time and grow into project management."

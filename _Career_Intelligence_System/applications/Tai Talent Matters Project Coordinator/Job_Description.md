# Project Coordinator / Project Officer — Tai Talent Matters

- **Recruiter:** Tai Talent Matters. The client is a telecom and infrastructure business (not named).
- **Reports to:** Head of Technical, Projects department
- **Deadline:** not stated

## About the role

Support the Head of Technical in delivering telecommunications and infrastructure projects for key client accounts. Keep projects moving day to day: plans and milestones, technical teams, subcontractors and suppliers, materials and site activities, and timely, accurate updates to clients and management. Hands-on, fast-paced, growing business. Office, project sites and client meetings. Path to project management.

## Key responsibilities

**1. Project coordination and delivery**
- Day-to-day activities from mobilisation through execution, testing, handover and close-out.
- Project schedules, work plans, milestone trackers and action logs.
- Track progress against plan, flag delays early, follow up.
- Line up resources, materials, tools and equipment ahead of site activities.
- Testing, commissioning, snagging and handover, with complete documentation.

**2. Technical and site coordination**
- Coordinate fibre, wireless, ICT, civil works and power deployments with engineers, supervisors and site teams.
- Site visits for progress, quality and compliance; follow up issues.
- Resolve bottlenecks; escalate technical issues to the Head of Technical.

**3. Client and stakeholder coordination**
- Day-to-day client contact on assigned accounts; regular progress updates.
- Minutes in client and internal meetings; follow through on actions.
- Work with finance, procurement, HSE and operations.
- Respond to client queries promptly; escalate concerns.

**4. Subcontractor and supplier coordination**
- Follow up subcontractors and suppliers on scope, timelines and quality.
- Track deliveries of materials and equipment; keep records current.
- Verify completed works; support certification, payment and close-out documents.

**5. Budget tracking, HSE and compliance**
- Track costs, purchase requests and spend against budget; flag variances.
- Log variations and change requests; route for approval.
- Permits, approvals, inspections and HSE documents in place before and during works.
- Risk and issue logs; support mitigation.

**6. Reporting and documentation**
- Weekly and monthly status reports: progress, costs, risks, issues.
- Organised project files, trackers and records.
- Completion and handover reports.

## Key performance areas

Milestones on time and in scope; accurate, timely trackers and reports; early escalation of risks, delays and cost variances; coordination of subcontractors, suppliers and site teams; HSE and quality compliance; client responsiveness.

## Qualifications and experience

- Bachelor's in Business, Project Management, Engineering, ICT or related.
- 3–5 years in a project coordination or project officer role, any sector.
- Start-up, scale-up or similarly fast-paced environment.
- **Advantage:** telecom, ICT or infrastructure (fibre, wireless, power, civil works).
- Comfortable with engineers; learns the technical side quickly.
- Working knowledge of scheduling, cost tracking and reporting.
- Strong Excel / Google Sheets. **Advantage:** MS Project, Asana, Monday, Jira or similar.
- **Advantage:** CAPM, PRINCE2 Foundation or PMP.
- Valid driving licence; willing to travel to sites.

## Competencies

Organisation, planning and detail; ownership and urgency; clear writing and speaking; stakeholder and vendor coordination; judgement on escalation; comfort with ambiguity and tight deadlines; basic commercial and cost awareness.

## Duties

- Technical support to onsite and remote users
- Install, configure and support (IMAC) end-user devices
- Manage/monitor Service Desk incidents and service requests in the call management system
- Manage user system access for various applications
- Improve service quality and team process efficiency
- Incidents to SLA; escalate outages to End User Services Team Leader
- Prioritise and allocate requests within IT Support teams

## Required

- 3–5 years in an IT support team
- Degree/Diploma in IT or related
- Incident management; call management system; Microsoft Office
- Windows 11 support and build (OS and PC hardware)
- Active Directory; Citrix support; SCCM; Exchange account maintenance
- Networking and patching

## Desirable

- ITIL Foundation certified; Microsoft certification; ServiceNow; Avaya telephony

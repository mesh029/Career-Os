# Recruitment Officer (US Timezone) — BruntWork

- **Source:** BruntWork careers portal (BW Internal Hiring), posted 27 Jan 2026, still open
- **Type:** Full time, remote, independent contractor (own taxes and benefits)
- **Shift:** Monday to Friday, 9:00 PM to 6:00 AM Manila time = **4:00 PM to 1:00 AM Kenya time**
- **Experience asked:** 1–3 years in the listing header; "at least four years in Recruitment or HR" in the requirements
- **Process:** Recruiter Assessment

## About the role

BruntWork places outsourced candidates with clients worldwide. Recruiters work on many client jobs at once, with an international team and one goal: bridge opportunities to the right outsourced candidate.

## Core responsibilities

- Source, screen and facilitate hiring of candidates who match client requirements: job description, pay and culture fit
- Secure clear commitments from candidates about job offers
- Work on multiple jobs at the same time
- Meet monthly hiring targets
- Follow recruitment standards on data privacy and protection
- Ensure a seamless, positive candidate experience

## Work setup

- Work from home with steady internet and power for uninterrupted video interviews and meetings
- No clock in or out, but responsive during the shift and hitting targets
- Uncapped incentives once targets are met

## Requirements

- At least four years in Recruitment or HR
- Fluent English, verbal and written
- Self-motivated, works well independently
- ATS experience (preferred, not required)
- Hits hiring targets and deadlines without losing quality
- Comfortable as an independent contractor
- Completes the Recruiter Assessment

## Benefits

Performance bonuses, HMO after 90 days (eligible locations), growth, immediate hiring.

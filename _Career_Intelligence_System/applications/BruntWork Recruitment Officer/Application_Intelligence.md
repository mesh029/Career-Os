# Application Intelligence — Recruitment Officer (US Timezone), BruntWork

*Packed 2026-10-08. Internal BruntWork hiring, remote, independent contractor. Listing open since 27 Jan 2026.*

## Verdict

**Apply. About 70%.** The angle is an IT professional who can match the right technical people to the right clients. BruntWork fills many IT roles, and a recruiter who can judge technical depth is rare. The hard gap is "at least four years in Recruitment or HR"; the CV doesn't hide it but leads with what transfers: training 80+ people, onboarding, mentoring, volume against deadlines, data privacy. The Recruiter Assessment matters more than the CV here, so prepare for it.

## Practical checks before applying

- **Shift:** 9 PM to 6 AM Manila = 4 PM to 1 AM Kenya. This clashes with the WorkinVirtual and Seamless Assist shifts and with a PATH day if you keep it. Decide which you can actually hold.
- **Internet and power:** they ask for steady internet and power for video calls. Have a backup (mobile data bundle, UPS or power bank for the router) before the assessment.
- **Contract:** independent contractor; you handle your own tax (KRA) and benefits.

## Salary

Placeholder **USD 600–800 a month** base plus uncapped incentives. If lower, ask about a performance review after 90 days.

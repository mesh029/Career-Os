# Application Intelligence: Data Entry Clerk, WorkinVirtual (remote)

**Submit:** `Meshack Ariri CV.pdf` + `Meshack Ariri Cover Letter.pdf` (this folder).
**Deadline:** 31 October 2026. Apply directly through WorkinVirtual.

## Is it real?

WorkinVirtual (Private) Limited is a small remote job board and recruiter registered in Pakistan (company registration H219002, per its terms of service). The domain dates from 2023 and ScamAdviser rates it "very likely safe". One public LinkedIn complaint (2025) about a broken application form and slow replies. Many of its listings forward to client employers, so the real employer may only be named later.

**Rules for this one:**
- Never pay for training, equipment, "processing" or a CV rewrite. A real job pays you.
- Do not send ID, bank or M-Pesa details until you have a written offer naming the employer.
- Do not install remote-access or "monitoring" software before an offer.
- If they move you to Telegram or WhatsApp-only chat with no company email, stop.

## Risks

- **Overqualified.** The CV is pitched as data entry and records, not health informatics, so it does not scare off a 1–3 year role. Keep interview answers in the same register.
- **Typing speed test likely.** Practise on a free typing test (aim 50+ WPM with 98%+ accuracy) and an Excel data-entry test before applying.
- **Hourly contractor.** Expect contractor terms, not employment. Confirm payment method (Payoneer, Wise, bank) and pay frequency.

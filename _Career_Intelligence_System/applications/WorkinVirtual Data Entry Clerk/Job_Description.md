## Summary

Enter, review, update, organise and maintain information across approved digital systems and records. Suited to someone careful, detail-focused, able to follow documented procedures, and who understands accurate and confidential information.

## Key responsibilities

- Enter information accurately into approved databases, spreadsheets and digital systems.
- Review source information before entering or updating records.
- Verify entered data for accuracy and completeness.
- Identify missing, inconsistent, duplicate or potentially incorrect information.
- Correct data-entry errors according to established procedures.
- Update existing records when new or corrected information becomes available.
- Maintain organised electronic records and files.
- Perform routine data-quality checks.
- Compare information across records when verification is required.
- Follow naming, formatting, classification and record-management standards.
- Prepare basic reports or summaries when required.
- Track incomplete records requiring additional information or follow-up.
- Maintain confidentiality; follow privacy, security and data-handling procedures.
- Communicate questions or discrepancies to the appropriate team member.
- Complete assigned work within agreed deadlines.

## Qualifications

- 1–3 years in data entry, information processing, records management, administrative support or similar.
- Attention to detail; accurate keyboard and data-entry skills; organisation.
- Follow written procedures consistently; spot discrepancies and incomplete information.
- Basic spreadsheet skills; comfortable with digital databases and online systems.
- Good written communication; manages repetitive tasks accurately; meets deadlines.
- Works independently remotely; sound judgment with confidential information.

## Preferred (any of)

Microsoft Excel, Google Sheets, Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, CRM systems, online databases, data validation, records management, document management, administrative systems, digital file organisation, basic reporting.

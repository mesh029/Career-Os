# Client Intake & Care Support Coordinator

**Employer:** Seamless Assist (staffing for a US home care agency client)
**Type:** Part-time, 20 hours/week, remote
**Pay:** USD 4–6 per hour
**Hours:** US Eastern business hours; strong availability late morning through early evening
**Listed location:** Nairobi, Kenya (remote)
**How to apply:** apply on the listing and describe experience with client-facing, detail-heavy coordination work

## About the role

Home care agency connecting families with background-checked caregivers for personal care, companionship and post-surgical support. Own fast, warm lead response and follow-up from first contact to booking, plus scheduling support and light billing and compliance work.

## Duties

- Monitor incoming leads and respond within minutes.
- Run a structured multi-touch follow-up cadence (call, text, email) over the first two weeks.
- Book qualified leads into the team calendar for calls and in-home visits.
- Ongoing empathetic follow-up with leads and clients.
- Manage rescheduling; keep the calendar accurate.
- Support recurring billing, invoicing and compliance reporting; keep records organised.
- Work in a secure, access-controlled account; HIPAA-compliant handling of client and health information.

## Required

- 2+ years in a healthcare-adjacent, caregiving or medical administration role (geriatric or cardiology a plus).
- Strong written and spoken English.
- Comfortable working without AI tools (no AI on client or health tasks).
- US Eastern business hours, late morning to early evening.
- Warm, unhurried phone presence while juggling leads.
- Detail-heavy recurring admin with steady accuracy.

## Preferred

- English and Spanish.
- Lead follow-up or intake coordination, ideally home care or healthcare.
- Lead or referral management platforms.
- Billing, invoicing and recurring compliance paperwork.

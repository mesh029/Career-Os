## Job overview

Review, organise, and optimise technology platforms used by the primary company and two partner initiatives. Not a content-creation seat. Evaluate the current set-up, correct configuration, and document it.

## Responsibilities

- **SimplePractice:** Review service codes, rates/fees, insurance settings, billing, forms, scheduling, communications, and other admin configuration
- **Stripe:** Review the existing account (payment links, deposits, plans, receipts, customer communications)
- **Stripe (nonprofit partner):** Help create and configure a separate Stripe account for donations / payments
- **Social scheduling platform:** Review scheduling, workflows, permissions, organisation, analytics
- **Social accounts:** Review profiles, business info, links, permissions, integrations
- **Websites:** Technical and functional review (missing, wrong, outdated, or improvable)
- **Integrations:** Review connections between platforms; streamline or automate where useful
- **Licence utilisation:** Check whether paid features are actually used
- **Documentation:** Basic docs of final configurations and processes so the team can maintain them

## Requirements

- Minimum 3 years in technology systems administration, platform configuration, or business process optimisation
- Proven expertise reviewing / auditing / improving configs across SimplePractice, Stripe, and social media management tools
- Strong understanding of systems integration, workflow automation, and data accuracy across platforms
- Demonstrated documentation and consistency across affiliated organisations
- HIPAA-trained with working knowledge of privacy/compliance for healthcare-related systems
- Excellent analytical, communication, and time management skills; independent remote work

## Overview

Shares responsibility for all equipment, applications and cloud services at Embassy Nairobi. Senior member of the ISC; frequent technical project manager and lead. Manages physical and virtual servers, routers, switches, cloud applications and services, mass storage, VPN connections, digital signage, AV solutions, peripherals and workstations; supports users locally and remotely.

## Duties

- **System administration and management (25%)**: proactive network monitoring, maximum uptime; with ENM, ITSC, Washington offices and Nairobi CIS: configure VPN devices, troubleshoot latency, monitor line availability and client switch configurations, coordinate failover to VPN alternate route.
- **User support (25%)**: second and third tier troubleshooting of hardware (PCs, printers) and software, including SharePoint, file shares, OneDrive and Office 365 tickets from the help desk coordinator; own, resolve, document steps in the ticket, close; outstanding customer service; guide users to the best tools.
- **IT consulting and technical project management (20%)**: lead IT consultant with end users; identify gaps and build solutions; introduce new tools; run tech talks; champion change; drive efficiency, oversight, transparency and interagency collaboration.
- **Mobile computing support (15%)**: set up tablets, phones, laptops, VR/AR headsets, mobile routers, PA systems and IoT devices; register and activate users on Department platforms; manage accounts and authentication for VPN access (GO Mobile, GO Desktop, GO Virtual, FAN.gov).
- **Systems engineering and design (10%)**: AV systems, virtual meeting rooms, conference centres, digital signage, DIN network architecture, American Center spaces; back-end support; gather requirements and prepare SOW and BOM with IRM management; recommend ITCCB-approved technology in line with Department security guidelines; review and recommend upgrades.
- **Other duties (5%)**.

## Requirements

- **Education:** university degree in Computer Science or Information Technology.
- **Experience:** at least **seven years** in a technical position involving LAN/WAN operations, management, administration and direct customer support; SharePoint administration; Power Automate.
- Must be able to obtain and hold the required clearance.

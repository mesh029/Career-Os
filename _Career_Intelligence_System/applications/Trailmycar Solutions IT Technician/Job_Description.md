## Job

Day-to-day IT for internal staff **and** inbound client calls on tracking systems. Keep tracking platforms and IT infrastructure up.

- Inbound client calls on tracking issues
- Log, troubleshoot, resolve or escalate to Operations for field
- Monitor tracking dashboards; flag offline or underperforming devices
- Trip reports, movement summaries, offline logs
- Internal hardware, software, network support
- User accounts, company devices, access
- Updates, antivirus, backups, IT security
- IT equipment, licences, credentials inventory
- Third-party IT vendors
- Train staff/clients on dashboards
- Ticket log and recurring issues
- Daily and weekly summaries: cases, device health, action taken

- Degree in IT/CS/related, **preferably JKUAT** (we are KCA: related field, not JKUAT)
- 1-2 years IT support or helpdesk (we have ~3)
- Tracking systems, fleet dashboards or CRM: **advantage**. CRM yes (Salesforce tickets). Fleet GPS: do not claim a telematics console. Dashboards and device-health monitoring yes.

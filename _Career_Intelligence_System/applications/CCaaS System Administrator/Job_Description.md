# Job Description — System Administrator (CCaaS & Enterprise Stack)

*Captured 2026-07-15 from user-provided posting. Employer name not specified — update when known.*

## Role summary

System Administrator serving as ultimate owner of digital resource optimization and technology framework. Critical pillar: complete ownership and optimization of **Contact Center as a Service (CCaaS)** infrastructure. Strategic, backend role focused on **IT auditing**, **software stack management**, and **high-level implementation** — not day-to-day tech support. Elite, autonomous executor; interacts with **executive leadership** and **operations heads** to drive ROI. Success measured by results, streamlined technology expenditure, and flawless system rollouts.

## Responsibilities

- Architect, optimize, and maintain **CCaaS environment**; ensure supporting modules — **Quality Assurance**, **Reporting/Analytics**, **Single Sign-On (SSO)**, and **Security protocols** — are fully integrated and running at peak performance.
- **Audit existing technology stack** continually to maximize tool utilization and eliminate unnecessary software.
- Build comprehensive **framework for all enterprise tools** to ensure optimal deployment and functionality across the organization.
- Develop clear **use-case plans** for proposed new tools; present to executive team for understanding, valuation, and approval.
- Coordinate **review and receipt of software vendor contracts**; prepare for final execution by the CEO.
- Design structural processes for **employee tool onboarding**; train tech support team and operations leadership on frontline execution.
- Create and review regular **audit reports on software usage**; guarantee only active, necessary users hold expensive licenses.
- Operate with **total autonomy** and minimal communication with non-leadership roles; full ownership of project lifecycles.

## Required skills & traits

- 3+ years significant experience in **systems administration**, **IT auditing**, or **software stack management**
- Deep expertise configuring, managing, and optimizing **enterprise CCaaS platforms** and ecosystems (QA, Reporting, SSO, Security)
- Proven track record managing **enterprise software lifecycles**, from **ROI analysis** to deployment
- Highly **autonomous** executor; zero micromanagement
- English fluent (written and verbal)
- High work ethic, professionalism, punctuality, reliability
- Proficient analyzing **tool utilization data** and **license management**
- Adapt quickly to changing business priorities; scale processes
- Meticulous attention to detail on **contracts** and **user access**
- Handle confidential company data and strategic plans with integrity
- Stay current with industry-leading enterprise tools
- Effective **time-management** and complex organizational skills
- Excellent analytical problem-solving and **executive presentation** skills

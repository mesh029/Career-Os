## Inferred scope (labelled as inferred, not a posted JD)

Drawn from CMMB volunteer FAQs, a 2022 Kenya **Administrative Officer / ICT Support** listing, and typical country-office health-NGO IT:

- First point of contact for staff, interns, and volunteers on IT issues
- Prepare and configure laptops with NY IT or a local vendor
- IT security awareness: new joiners complete training; chase overdue modules
- Post and track IT policies (intranet / sign-off)
- Asset tagging, inventory, assignment records
- Microsoft 365 / email / identity for a small country office
- Support partner facilities (historically Mutomo and CHAMPS counties) where connectivity and devices are thin
- Optional overlap with **e-Health**: digital tools used in MNCH programmes (do not claim ECHIS unless you have it)

A full site JD is usually sent **after** the first screening interview.

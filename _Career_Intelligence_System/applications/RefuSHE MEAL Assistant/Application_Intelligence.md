## Watch-outs

- **Reference number needed.** The advert asks for title and reference number in the subject. RefuSHE numbers roles RK/HR/REC26-xx; get the MEAL Assistant number from the same table you found RK/HR/REC26-12 in.
- **Certificates.** The advert asks for copies; you are sending CV and cover letter only. Keep the degree and DHIS2 Academy certificate ready if HR asks.
- **Field travel.** Expect visits to where refugee girls and young women live in Nairobi. Confirm transport arrangements.
- **Salary.** About KSh 54K average at RefuSHE (MyJobMag, one report). An Assistant-level MEAL post may pay less.
- **Fees.** Never pay for notarisation, certificates or assessments.

## Likely interview questions

1. You find the same girl registered twice with different spellings of her name. What do you do?
2. How would you set up a Kobo form for attendance so the data comes out by gender, age, nationality, disability and location?
3. During data collection a girl tells you something that worries you about her safety. What happens next?
4. How do you check data quality without slowing programme staff down?
5. A donor report is due Friday and a third of the attendance data is missing. What do you do?
6. How do you take consent for a photo or a success story from a young woman, or from a minor?
7. Show us how you would summarise attendance for a quarter in Excel.

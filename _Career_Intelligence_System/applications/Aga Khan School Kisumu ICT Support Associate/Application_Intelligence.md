## Likely interview questions

1. **"A teacher's projector and laptop fail five minutes before a lesson. What do you do?"** Triage by impact. Get the lesson running first with a spare device or a quick fix, log the ticket, then find the root cause after class.
2. **"How would you secure student data?"** Accounts with MFA and least privilege; patched, protected endpoints; backups; the Kenya Data Protection Act; staff awareness; no shared passwords; device handover procedures.
3. **"Walk us through setting up a new access point."** Mount and power it (PoE), adopt it in the controller, assign SSIDs and VLANs (staff, students, guest), set security (WPA2/WPA3 Enterprise for staff), check coverage and roaming, document it.
4. **"How do you keep ICT inventory accurate?"** Asset tags, a register with user, location and condition, updates at onboarding and offboarding, periodic audits, and a repair log with vendors.
5. **"Why a school, after a health organisation?"** Same job: keep people working and data safe. A school is a stable, local, mission-driven place where ICT directly affects learning. AKDN values match.

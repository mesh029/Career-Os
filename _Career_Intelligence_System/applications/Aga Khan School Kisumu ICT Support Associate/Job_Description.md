## The position

The successful candidate delivers effective ICT support across the school, keeps the digital infrastructure running smoothly, and helps staff and students get the most out of technology in teaching, learning and daily operations.

## Functional skills and attributes

**1. Technical proficiency**
- Install, configure and maintain ICT hardware, software and networks.
- Sound knowledge of cybersecurity best practice and data protection compliance.
- Manage ICT assets, keep an accurate inventory and coordinate equipment repairs.
- Strong user support: develop ICT procedures, deliver training, promote effective use of technology.
- Familiarity with server infrastructure and wireless access point management (e.g. Aruba networks).

**2. Interpersonal and communication**
- Train and build capacity among non-technical users.
- Communicate well and work effectively with internal teams and external vendors.
- Professional, proactive and responsive service.

**3. Analytical and problem-solving**
- Careful, accurate diagnosis and resolution of ICT issues.
- Prioritise multiple tasks and respond well to emergencies.

## Requirements

- **Qualifications:**
  - Diploma in Computer Science, IT or a related field; a Bachelor's degree is an added advantage.
  - Relevant certifications such as CCNA, Microsoft Systems Engineering or ITIL.
- **Experience:**
  - At least 2 years in a similar role; experience in a school environment is an added advantage.
  - Proven experience managing network infrastructure and user support in an institutional setting.

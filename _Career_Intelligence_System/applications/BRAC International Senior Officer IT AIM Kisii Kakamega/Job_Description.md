# Senior Officer, IT, AIM (Kisii / Kakamega)

**Employer:** BRAC International, Mastercard Foundation AIM programme (Accelerating Impact for Young Women), Education Pathway
**Location:** Kisii (Kisii County) / Kakamega, Kenya. Full time
**Deadline:** 9 October 2026
**How to apply:** Trakstar Hire form on the BRAC International vacancy ("Senior officer IT-Kisii/Kakamega"). Upload resume (all job assignments in detail) and cover letter

## Job summary

Comprehensive IT support to the Regional Office(s) and branches in the assigned region(s): first-level support for hardware, end-user devices, software, internet and network connectivity, and business applications used by the Regional Office, branches and programme teams. Timely resolution, user support and documentation in line with BI Technology standards.

## Responsibilities

- First-line support for country business applications (BInsight, sbiCloud, Orbit, etc.).
- Coordinate with the Senior Manager, IT to log, monitor and resolve user issues.
- Triage complex issues not resolvable on site; escalate to HO IT, product management or programme with detailed documentation and root-cause analysis through Jira.
- Stable, secure connectivity across offices: routers, switches, wireless access points; diagnose and resolve network issues.
- Install, configure and maintain laptops, desktops, printers, scanners and other equipment; endpoint protection, antivirus and patch management.
- Strong relationships with end users; understand business needs; manage expectations.
- Primary liaison between field, programme and Country IT.
- Accurate records of support activities in ticketing and knowledge base systems.
- In-person training on applications and best practices; technical onboarding of new employees at their location.
- Establish and enforce IT policies, procedures and best practices.

## Experience

- 2–3 years in technical support, application support or field service.
- Software troubleshooting, configuration, application error handling and application support.
- International NGO / development sector highly preferred.
- Cloud-based systems, data governance, digital transformation.

## Knowledge and skills

Application troubleshooting; networking basics (TCP/IP, LAN/WAN, firewalls); Windows, macOS, Linux; problem solving; explaining technical concepts to non-technical users; stakeholder management across cultures; organisation; integrity; change management and adoption; extensive travel.

First name, last name, email, phone, resume (file), LinkedIn profile, cover letter (file), expected gross monthly salary, willing to relocate.

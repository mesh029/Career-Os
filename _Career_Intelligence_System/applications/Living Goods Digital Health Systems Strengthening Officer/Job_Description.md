## The work

Technical assistance to county and sub-county health teams for government-led community health digital transformation.

- **30%** Capacity building: governance structures, train and mentor government staff, policy and SOP recommendations, job aids, peer learning.
- **15%** Coordination and reporting: CHMT and sub-county meetings, partner alignment, weekly technical reports, adoption of national standards.
- **10%** Data quality and performance: device sync rates, data verification, performance issues, data use, regional dashboards.
- **25%** Change, incident and problem management: controlled rollout of updates, incident process, incident logs and known errors, root cause sessions.
- **10%** Devices and assets: government ICT asset guidelines, a device registry, basic troubleshooting training, lifecycle, security and connectivity.
- **10%** Improvement: automation, user-support ideas, pilots, UAT feedback, setup of new systems inside government structures.

## Requirements

Bachelor's in Informatics, Computer Science, or a related field. Technical certifications preferred.

Competencies: systems thinking, strategic thinking, stakeholder management, facilitation, data analysis, work planning.

## Organisation background

Founded in 2011, INSO provides free safety coordination services to registered NGOs in 26 insecure countries — incident tracking, analytical reports, safety data/mapping, crisis management support, orientations, and training.

## Overall purpose

Provide IT and application support to all INSO offices and implement projects around organisational IT standardisation and compliance. Travel to country missions for hands-on project delivery. Act as first-line support during visits and second-line support remotely. Bridge HQ IT and country offices.

## Main duties

- On-demand hardware, software, and installation support via TeamViewer and Microsoft Teams
- Travel to Francophone country and satellite offices for IT policy compliance reviews and project work
- IT support focal point between HQ IT and country offices
- Ad-hoc training on INSO IT technologies and policies (**French and English**)
- Review country-office IT infrastructure; upgrade/replace/configure to INSO standards
- Assist with SharePoint site management, permissions, and usage training
- In-person or remote IT onboarding for new hires
- Install/configure computers to INSO standards (OS and applications)
- Develop tutorials for INSO IT tools and services
- Assist with cybersecurity and IT awareness guidelines

## Essential requirements

- 2–4 years professional IT support experience
- Humanitarian (INGO) context experience, including frequent travel and potentially high-risk countries
- Windows + Microsoft stack: Entra ID, M365, Office 365, Exchange Online, SharePoint Online
- Cybersecurity awareness and practices
- Microsoft Intune deployment and device management
- Network concepts and Wi-Fi troubleshooting
- **Professional working proficiency in written and spoken French and English**
- Eligibility and ability to travel frequently across INSO countries
- IT-related degree or equivalent practical experience
- Experience in fully remote teams with predominantly remote customers

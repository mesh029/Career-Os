## Purpose

Support MEL during IRS: high-quality data collection, verification and reporting through the pre-spray, spray and post-spray phases.

## Key responsibilities

### Pre-spray
- Support training of spray operators and team leaders on MEL tools, indicators and mobile data collection platforms.
- Set up IRS phones and tablets: SIM activation, charging, application installation.
- Help set up mobile DHIS2 and other digital platforms before training and launch.
- Review geographies so sprayer teams are correctly assigned daily.
- Troubleshoot smartphones and syncing configurations.

### During spray
- Supervise correct use of phones and MEL tools at spray sites.
- Verify daily data at point of collection for accuracy, completeness and GPS capture.
- Mentor field teams on accurate collection and project indicators.
- Support site coordinators to update the spray performance tracker daily.
- Track IRS sticker use and availability; report shortages to the Sub County MEL.
- Resolve data errors and device syncing problems.
- Monitor real-time data flow; weekly progress reports at site and sub-county level.
- Attend weekly sub-county review meetings; compile summaries for the MEL Manager.
- Join community data verification and household follow-up when discrepancies arise.

### Post-spray
- Retrieve and clear devices with team leaders and site supervisors; all data synced before storage.
- Final syncing and secure return of phones and tablets to the main store.
- Resolve remaining data issues: duplicates, GPS errors, household detail corrections.
- Support final analysis and interpretation of spray data.
- Contribute to end-of-campaign reporting and debriefs.

## Requirements

- Bachelor's in Biostatistics, Statistics, Public Health, Epidemiology, Data Science, Computer Science, ICT or related + 2 years relevant experience (1 year in IRS preferred), OR HND + 2 years, OR Diploma + 6 years.
- At least 1 year in MEL, data entry supervision or mobile-based data collection.
- ODK, Kobo Toolbox or DHIS2.
- Field data collection supervision or large-scale health interventions desirable.
- DHIS2 Academy course an added advantage.
- MS Excel, Word, database applications; spray data analysis and interpretation.
- Communication, teamwork, pressure and deadlines.

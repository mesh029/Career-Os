## Likely interview questions

1. What do you check when you verify a spray form at the point of collection? Completeness of required fields, structure and room counts that make sense (sprayed not more than found), household details, GPS captured, correct team and area, submitted and synced.
2. A spray operator's phone will not sync at end of day. Steps? Check network and data bundle, unsent forms, app and server settings, storage, restart; keep the phone until data is safe; escalate if data is at risk; never delete unsent forms.
3. How do you train operators who are new to phones? Short demo, one form together, practice with dummy households, a one-page job aid, then check their first real submissions the next morning.
4. The tracker shows one team far behind target. What do you do? Check if it is a data gap (unsynced) or a real coverage gap; talk to the team leader; report it in the daily and weekly summary.
5. Why IRS again? You have done one campaign, you understand the data chain, and you want the field side of MEL where data quality is won or lost.

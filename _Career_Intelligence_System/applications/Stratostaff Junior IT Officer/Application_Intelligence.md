## Update 7 Oct 2026: full advert

- **The client is in oil and gas, in Nairobi.** The cover letter now ties PATH downtime pressure to an operation where downtime costs money by the hour. Do not claim oil and gas, SCADA or OT experience.
- **It's a contract, not permanent.** Ask the length, whether it can be renewed, who pays you (Stratostaff or the client) and whether the pay is gross or net.
- **They want someone available immediately.** You are still at PATH, so know your real notice period before the call. If asked, give the true date you could start. Offering to negotiate a shorter notice with PATH is fine; promising "tomorrow" is not.
- **Weigh it against PATH.** A junior contract role is a step down in title and probably in security. Only take it if the pay and contract length make sense.

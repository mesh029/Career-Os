# Apply — Junior IT Officer, Stratostaff (Nairobi)

Apply on Stratostaff: https://jobs.stratostaff.co.ke

The MyJobMag page is only the advert. Use the Stratostaff link. Never pay for notarisation, a certificate, an assessment or "processing".

## Which route

The same advert is on LinkedIn under **Cleave global e services**. Stratostaff posted it first, with its own apply portal, so apply there.

Before using LinkedIn Easy Apply as well, open the LinkedIn post and check "Meet the hiring team":
- **The poster works at Stratostaff:** Easy Apply is fine too; it reaches the same recruiter.
- **The poster works at Cleave or is unknown:** skip Easy Apply. The same client getting your CV from two agencies can disqualify you.

## Fields

**Position:** Junior IT Officer

**Location:** Nairobi. Willing to work on site in Nairobi.

**Highest qualification:** Bachelor's degree, BSc Applied Computing, KCA University, November 2023

**KCSE grade:** B+ and above. On the form, enter the exact grade and year printed on the certificate.

**Years of experience:** 3

**Current title:** Health Informatics / HMIS Officer, PATH (Mar 2025 to present)

**Notice period / availability:** The advert says "Immediately". Give your real PATH notice, e.g. "Available after [X] weeks' notice; can negotiate earlier." Do not promise an immediate start you cannot keep.

**Contract:** Accept that it's a contract. Ask the length and renewal terms at the interview, not on the form.

**Expected salary:** Leave blank if you can. If the form forces a number, use a Nairobi junior IT range you would actually accept, and do not use the KSh 19,000 figure on the MyJobMag sidebar. That average is not this role.

**Skills box, if they give you a short list:** First-line IT support, Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, user accounts, IT asset register

## Safety

The employer behind Stratostaff is not named. Before an offer, ask who the client is, who pays you, and whether the role is permanent or a fixed contract. No ID or bank details until the offer is in writing.

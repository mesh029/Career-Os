**Tableau?**
Same job as Power BI: take a cleaned dataset, build the view managers actually use, check it against source before it goes into a pack.

**BInsight?**
I have not used BInsight. I have implemented and supported DHIS2, KenyaEMR and Salesforce, plus Excel and Kobo as the manual layer, and Power BI and Tableau for the views. I onboard users, chase late entry, and make the dashboard match what is reported. That is the job. The product name changes.

**How do you keep MIS numbers matching programme reports?**
I do not start from the dashboard. I start from how the field enters. Then I clean, then I check a sample against source (register, tracker, EMR), then I publish. If the dashboard and the narrative disagree, the dashboard is wrong until I can explain the gap.

**Training?**
I write the job aid, I run the session, I sit with the person who still enters late. 80+ staff. The test is whether next month's data arrives on time without me chasing every row.

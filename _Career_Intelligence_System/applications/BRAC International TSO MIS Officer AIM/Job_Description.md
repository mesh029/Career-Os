# Technical Sector Officer, MIS (TSO-MIS) Officer, AIM — BRAC International

*Captured 2026-09-16 from BRAC International / Trakstar Hire. Nairobi, full-time. AIM (Mastercard Foundation Accelerating Impact for Young Women).*

## Programme

AIM equips 2 million adolescent girls and young women (2021-2030) across seven African countries including Kenya. This post sits on the **AIM Education pathway**: access and completion of secondary education for girls 12-17. Digital MIS is **BInsight**, plus a **manual** MIS. Country officer works with BI HO T4D, BI HO IT, and the AIM central team.

## What they actually hire for

A country **programme MIS owner**: implement and improve digital and manual MIS, train staff, keep BInsight numbers matching what is reported, clean data, build dashboards, run data quality checks, support MEL audits, troubleshoot, escalate in **JIRA**, back up hard copy and digital sources.

## Requirements that matter

- 3+ years digital **and** manual MIS / database management in NGO/INGO
- Structure datasets for analysis; "BIG data" in the programme sense
- MIS technical assistance, training module development, training facilitation
- Cultural sensitivity; integrated social development programming is a plus
- Hardware/software literacy
- Platforms named: Taroworks-Salesforce, CommCare, **Power BI**, ONA, Tableau
- Excel, PowerPoint, Word
- Stata/SPSS an advantage, not a gate
- English fluency

## Summary
Bachelor’s in IT/CS/related + 1–2 years ICT support or sysadmin. First-level technical support and admin assistance for university ICT infrastructure: availability, security, efficiency.

## Key responsibilities
1. **Service Desk & User Support** — walk-in/phone/email/ticketing; L1 incidents within SLA (95% high-priority within 4 hours); onboarding/offboarding; ERP (Finance/Staff/Student) user assistance; security protocols.
2. **Infrastructure Monitoring & Resilience** — monitor network/power/data center dashboards (99.5% availability); daily health checks servers/printers/PCs/AV; escalate critical alerts within 30 min; backup/DR logs.
3. **Security & Compliance** — SOC alert monitoring & escalate; AV definitions & patches; data protection (encryption, access logging) vs Kenya DPA / research funding; physical ICT asset security.
4. **Asset & Inventory** — ICT Asset Registry (100% tagged); EOL identification for renewal; software license tracking (Zoom, M365).
5. **Innovation & Teaching Support** — innovation hub / hackathons / demos (network, cloud); AV setup for faculty training; paperless procurement troubleshooting.
6. **Documentation & Reporting** — ticket logs; semester ICT Resource Utilization Report inputs; SOPs / knowledge base.

## Requirements
- 1–2 years ICT support or system administration  
- Bachelor’s IT / CS / BIT or equivalent  
- Windows 10/11 & Microsoft 365 Admin Center  
- Basic networking (TCP/IP, DNS, DHCP, VLANs)  
- Ticketing (Jira, ServiceDesk, Zendesk, etc.)  
- ERP familiarity (Dynamics/SAP/Oracle) — advantage  
- Cybersecurity basics (firewalls, AV, phishing)

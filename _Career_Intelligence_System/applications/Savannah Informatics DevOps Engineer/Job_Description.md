## What they say the job is

Interface of development and operations across planning, building, testing, deployment and support. They want someone familiar with Ruby or Python. They also say lack of work experience is not a handicap if the skills, attitude and learning speed are real, and they welcome unconventional backgrounds.

## Responsibilities (their list)

Customer requirements and KPIs. Development, testing, automation tools and infrastructure. Team planning and project management. Stakeholders. Setting up tools and infrastructure. DevOps processes for development, test, release, update and support. Review and validate code. Troubleshooting and bug fixes. Process monitoring and improvement. Automation. Vulnerability assessment and risk management. Incident management and root cause analysis. Team and customer communication. Choosing CI/CD tools and building the pipeline. Mentoring. Customer-experience and KPI monitoring. Progress reports to management and the customer.

## Skills they list

1. Linux / Ubuntu / Debian and a UNIX shell
2. Networks: OSI, TCP/IP, UDP, ICMP, HTTP(S), DNS, DHCP, SMTP
3. Docker, LXD or Containerd, and production applications on Kubernetes
4. Git plus GitLab / GitHub / Bitbucket. CI/CD: GitLab CI, GitHub Actions, CircleCI. PostgreSQL
5. GCP and AWS: GCE/EC2, Cloud Run/EBS, Cloud Functions/Lambda, GKE/EKS, S3/GCS, PubSub/SQS
6. Ansible playbooks. Terraform and/or Pulumi, CloudFormation
7. Python (Django) and Golang. REST, newer apps on GraphQL. NGINX in front of the APIs. Go services sometimes exposed directly
8. Debug end to end, write RCAs, monitoring stack plus Sentry
9. Willingness to learn the whole stack

## Benefits

Mission, healthcare impact, growth, market salary, health and medical. Background check for final candidates.

# Technical Field Support — Value Chain Factory (Evergreen Requisition)

- **Employer:** Value Chain Factory (applications on the Integr8 careers portal)
- **Location:** Nairobi, Kenya. Physical, with nationwide field travel
- **Closes:** 31 Dec 2026 (evergreen requisition, so hiring may be rolling)

## About the job

Support the rollout, installation, configuration, maintenance and technical support of a hardware-dependent solution across multiple sites. Keep deployed hardware, peripherals, connectivity and related systems working through implementation and post-deployment. Hands-on field support, troubleshooting, user training, asset records, and vendor and site coordination.

## Duties

**A. Hardware deployment and site installation**
- Deploy, install, configure, test and commission hardware at assigned sites.
- Pre-deployment site readiness checks: power, connectivity, space, security, user availability.
- Install and configure computers, tablets, printers, scanners, biometric devices, routers, switches, power backup units and other equipment.
- Test, tag, document and hand over devices per procedure.
- Hardware movement, relocation, replacement and retrieval.

**B. Technical support and maintenance**
- First- and second-line support to users and site teams.
- Diagnose and resolve hardware faults, connectivity issues, peripheral failures, power problems and user incidents.
- Routine preventive maintenance.
- Coordinate repairs, replacements, warranty claims and vendor support.
- Escalate unresolved issues.

**C. User support and training**
- Hands-on guidance on devices and systems.
- Train users on basic troubleshooting, safe handling, device care and reporting.
- Simple user guides, support notes, checklists and FAQs.
- Promote responsible use and accountability for hardware.

**D. Asset management and documentation**
- Accurate register of hardware, accessories, spares, consumables and assigned equipment.
- Record serial numbers, sites, assigned users, deployment dates, maintenance history and status.
- Installation, site visit, maintenance and incident reports; handover documentation.
- Audits, asset verification, stock reconciliation and reporting.

**E. Connectivity, power and field operations**
- Basic network setup and troubleshooting: routers, switches, access points, modems, LAN, Wi-Fi, internet.
- Liaise with network providers, site representatives and technical teams.
- Identify and report power issues: unstable power, missing backup, cabling risks, surge protection gaps.
- Nationwide field visits; feedback to improve rollout planning.

**F. Compliance, security and quality**
- Installations comply with technical standards, security requirements and procedures.
- Basic device security: passwords, access restrictions, updates, endpoint protection, safe data handling.
- Report misuse, loss, theft, damage, tampering or unauthorised relocation.
- Post-installation checks to confirm sites are operational.

## Skills and competencies

- Strong troubleshooting and problem-solving; good communication and user support; high attention to detail in documentation and asset tracking.
- Works independently in the field; trains non-technical users; integrity and accountability.
- Willing to travel extensively; flexible hours during rollout, maintenance or emergencies.

## Qualifications

- Degree in IT, Computer Science, Computer Engineering, Telecommunications, Electrical/Electronic Engineering or related.
- 3–5 years in technical support, hardware maintenance, field operations, IT support or technology deployment.
- Practical experience supporting hardware-dependent solutions across multiple sites.
- **Advantage:** large-scale rollouts, government systems, access control, biometric systems, POS, field service operations or distributed technology environments.
- **Advantage:** CompTIA A+, Network+, CCNA, Microsoft certification, ITIL Foundation or equivalent.

## Likely interview questions

1. **You arrive at a site and there's no stable power. What do you do?** Don't install on bad power. Record it with photos, report it to the project lead and site, recommend surge protection or a UPS, and reschedule or install with protection if approved.
2. **How do you keep an asset register accurate across 30 sites?** Tag on arrival, record the serial, site, user and date at handover, sign handover forms, reconcile on every visit, log moves and repairs the same day.
3. **A device fails after deployment. Walk us through it.** Remote triage first, then on-site test, swap from spares to restore service, then the warranty or vendor claim, and update the register and incident log.
4. **How do you train users who aren't technical?** Show, let them do it, give a one-page guide, check back on the next visit.
5. **Are you comfortable travelling for weeks?** Yes. Ask about per diem, transport and accommodation arrangements.

## Practical questions for the interview

- How many sites, which counties, how long the rollout runs.
- Contract type and length (project-based is likely).
- Travel allowances, transport, accommodation.
- What the hardware solution is (government system? biometric?).

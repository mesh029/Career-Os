## Watch-outs

- **Reference number: RK/HR/REC26-12.** From RefuSHE's vacancy table (IT Assistant, 1 position); MyJobMag leaves it out. Subject: "IT Assistant – RK/HR/REC26-12".
- **Certificates.** The advert asks for copies; you are sending CV and cover letter only. Have the degree, CCNA, AZ-900 and Google IT Support certificates ready to send the same day if HR asks.
- **Salary.** MyJobMag shows an average of about KSh 54K at RefuSHE (one report). Decide your floor before the interview.
- **Contract.** Ask about length, renewal and probation.
- **Safeguarding.** Expect a safeguarding policy, code of conduct and background checks. Answer with how you protect data about girls and children.
- **CCNA.** Confirm whether it is the Cisco exam certification or the NetAcad course before the interview.
- **Fees.** Never pay for notarisation, certificates or assessments.

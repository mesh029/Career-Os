## About RefuSHE

In 2008 Anne and Talyn launched RefuSHE, then Heshima Kenya, as an innovative solution within the global refugee crisis: an organisation built on the principle that every young woman deserves opportunity. RefuSHE provides the full spectrum of care and protection for refugee girls, young women and children in Kenya.

## Purpose of the role

The IT Assistant keeps RefuSHE's computers, network, phones and systems working, secure and available, so staff can work without interruption. The role gives day-to-day technical support to staff, looks after IT equipment and user accounts, and helps protect the sensitive data RefuSHE holds about the girls, young women and families it serves.

## Key responsibilities

- Give first-line IT support to staff in person, by phone and remotely, and log and resolve requests on time.
- Set up, configure and maintain laptops, desktops, printers, phones, projectors and other IT equipment.
- Install, update and support operating systems, software and approved applications.
- Set up and manage user accounts, email, passwords and access rights for new, moving and departing staff.
- Look after the office network, Wi-Fi, internet connection and routers, and follow up with internet service providers when there are problems.
- Make sure antivirus, security updates and backups are in place and working.
- Help protect data and systems by applying IT security policies and spotting and reporting phishing, viruses or data breaches.
- Train staff on IT basics, safe use of systems, password security and data protection.
- Keep an up-to-date IT asset register, including serial numbers, users and condition, and help with asset checks.
- Prepare equipment for new staff and recover and wipe equipment from departing staff.
- Set up IT equipment and audio-visual support for meetings, trainings and events, including online meetings.
- Advise on IT purchases and prepare specifications for new equipment, working with the procurement team.
- Manage IT supplier and service contracts, such as internet, software licences and repairs, and track renewal dates.
- Keep simple records of IT support requests, problems and fixes.
- Escalate complex problems to the supervisor or external IT service providers.
- Carry out other related duties assigned by the supervisor.

## Qualifications

- A diploma or degree in Information Technology, Computer Science or a related field.
- At least 2 years of experience in IT support, ideally in an NGO or office setting.
- Hands-on experience with Windows and macOS, Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace, and common office software.
- Able to set up and troubleshoot laptops, printers, phones, local networks and Wi-Fi.
- Basic knowledge of IT security, backups and antivirus tools.
- Knowledge of data protection principles and the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019.
- Certifications such as CompTIA A+, Network+ or Microsoft 365 are an advantage.
- Good communication skills and patient when helping staff with different levels of IT skill.
- Well organised, with good record-keeping.
- Honest, discreet and able to handle confidential information.
- Fluent spoken and written English and Kiswahili.
- Committed to gender equality, inclusion and safeguarding.

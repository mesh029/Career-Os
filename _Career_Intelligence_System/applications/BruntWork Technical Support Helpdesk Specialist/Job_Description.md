## Overview

Our client is looking for a Technical Support & Helpdesk Specialist (Hardware & SaaS) who will manage the day-to-day resolution of inbound support tickets, both client-facing and internal, ensuring a high standard of service delivery and adherence to SLAs. The role sits within the Global Customer Support team and plays a key part in ensuring clients and internal stakeholders receive accurate and professional support while helping to identify recurring issues and improve support processes.

## Responsibilities

### Helpdesk & Support

- Lodge, triage and manage inbound support tickets — both client-facing and internal, via Zendesk and Asana in line with SLA requirements
- Coordinate between internal teams (data, technical, client success) to resolve issues and escalate where necessary
- Keep clients and stakeholders updated throughout the ticket lifecycle, proactively chasing internal teams for progress where needed
- Maintain clear and accurate ticket documentation throughout the lifecycle of each issue
- Manage and control ticket queues to ensure timely resolution within SLA targets
- Respond to enquiries via web and email, with phone-based support required as needed
- Keep Zendesk and Asana records current and accurate at all times
- Contribute to the development and upkeep of helpdesk resources including troubleshooting guides and templates
- Contribute positively to team culture and cross-functional collaboration
- Actively participate in team meetings, training, and development activities

### Level 1 Device Troubleshooting

- Triage, investigate, and resolve client issues relating to hardware (sensors and cameras), data, and platform access
- Perform first-line troubleshooting and remote resets for connected devices before escalating to the Technical team
- Guide clients through basic device troubleshooting steps over phone, email, or web chat
- Identify recurring hardware or connectivity issues and flag patterns to the technical and data teams

### General

- Maintain superior levels of service standards to ensure the highest level of client satisfaction
- Adhere to operational procedures (meetings, punctuality, leave procedures)
- Interact positively with staff within and outside the division
- Use company equipment according to procedure and report damage
- Give, receive and act on feedback
- Other relevant duties as directed

## Requirements

- 1–3 years of experience in a helpdesk, technical support, or customer service role
- Strong written and verbal communication skills
- Experience with helpdesk or ticketing platforms — Zendesk and Asana preferred
- Ability to manage competing priorities and work across time zones in a global team
- Strong problem-solving; works independently and across teams
- Able to communicate by phone as well as web and email
- High attention to detail and accurate records
- SaaS or technology-related environment preferred

## Purpose

Own laptops, accounts, network, licences. Central IT. Speed of resolution plus protection of devices, licences, access, and data.

## Responsibilities

- Live asset register: holder, condition, repair history, replacement date
- Software licensing: cost, renewal, actual use; unused before renew; unlicensed systems
- Helpdesk end to end: log, SLA, escalate; joiner setup and leaver recovery same day
- Network and WiFi: guest vs company, first call, ISP liaison
- Accounts and access: join / change / leave; no leftover access; every account tied to a person
- Security: endpoint protection, patching, backups, MFA; respond when something looks wrong; Data Protection Act 2019

## Success measures (baseline then after)

Helpdesk turnaround down. Asset audit matches register. Licence spend clean. Network uptime up. Access review clean. No repeat security finding.

## Required

- 3+ years IT admin / sysadmin / helpdesk with ownership
- Windows/macOS and M365 or Google Workspace
- TCP/IP, DNS, DHCP, VPN; routers, switches, WiFi APs
- Endpoint protection, firewalls, backups, access control
- Clear written English

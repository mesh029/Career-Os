# Network Administrator — Gertrude's Children's Hospital

*Captured 2026-09-14 from MyJobMag Kenya. Posted 14 Sep 2026. Deadline **20 September 2026**. Nairobi. Full time. Apply at **apps.gerties.org**.*

## Employer

Not-for-profit paediatric hospital, established 1947. The most established children's hospital in Eastern and Central Africa. **15 facilities** in and around Nairobi, 300,000+ outpatients a year, 100-bed main facility at Muthaiga.

This is a healthcare employer running clinical systems across many sites. That is the same shape of environment as PATH.

## Role

Responsible for **availability, performance, capacity and integrity** of the hospital-wide network:

- Local and wide area networks, internet, **wireless services**, **inter-site links**
- **IP telephony**
- Connectivity to **cloud and partner-hosted systems**
- Keeping **clinical and enterprise systems accessible around the clock across all facilities**
- Implementing and operating **network security controls defined with the Cyber Security team**
- Providing the network foundation for new **clinical, biomedical and IoT** technologies
- Working with the ICT team, **clinical departments**, service providers and vendors
- Delivering a **resilient, well documented and scalable** network that supports safe, high quality care

## Qualifications, experience and skills

- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Systems or related field
- **At least 3 years' experience as a Network Administrator**
- **CCNA, Fortinet NSE, or an equivalent industry recognised networking certification**

# Application Intelligence — TELUS Digital Personalized Internet Assessor (KE)

## Verdict

**Apply. ~88% eligible; the exam is the real filter.** Low pay, low effort, flexible hours. It runs alongside a full-time job and the Seamless Assist evenings without conflict.

## Is it real?

- TELUS Digital (formerly TELUS International AI, which absorbed Lionbridge AI) is a real, large search-rating vendor. The "Personalized Internet Assessor" program has run for years in many countries.
- `telusinternational.ai` is their real domain. Scammers copy TELUS. Real TELUS **never** asks for a fee, never sells "exam answers" or "account activation", and never hires through WhatsApp or Telegram alone.
- Ignore anyone offering to "sell" or "rent" a TELUS account. That breaks the contract and gets you banned.

- Pay is per task, so speed with accuracy raises the effective hourly rate. Slow, careless work lowers it, and quality scores can end the contract.
- Pocket money with a small CV upside: "search quality rating for a global search engine" reads well next to data-quality and HMIS work.

## What "Personalized" means

- Some tasks rate results **for you**, using your own Google account and activity, not just general relevance. Expect to log in with your personal Gmail on the phone and computer you register.
- Read the privacy terms and the project guidelines before agreeing. If you are not comfortable sharing account activity with the project, this variant is not for you.
- Use a Gmail account you have actually used for a while, with real history. Do not create a new one for this.

## Exclusivity: the clause that matters

- Once you submit, you are locked out of other TELUS AI Community roles until you are rejected or you leave.
- If you are eyeing another TELUS project (for example Online Data Analyst or a different rater project), pick one first.
- Rater contracts usually also bar working on a similar search-rating project for another vendor at the same time. Check the contract before signing if you join Appen, RaterLabs or similar.
- Ordinary jobs (PATH, BRAC, Seamless Assist, etc.) are unaffected.

## The exam (the real filter)

Usually three parts: theory on the guidelines, practical rating tasks, and a language test. You get the guidelines first, and it is open-book, but strict on judgment. The time window is stated in the exam email (often about a week); read it carefully.

**How to pass**

1. **Read the full guidelines before opening the exam.** Google's public *Search Quality Rater Guidelines* PDF is the basis; TELUS adds its own project rules. Read both.
2. **Learn the two scales cold.**
   - *Needs Met:* Fully Meets / Highly / Moderately / Slightly / Fails to Meet. "Fully Meets" is rare: it is only for specific queries with one clear answer, such as an official site.
   - *Page Quality:* Lowest to Highest, judged on E-E-A-T (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trust).
3. **YMYL ("Your Money or Your Life")** pages on health, money, safety, law or news get the strictest standard. Your health-data background helps here.
4. **Think like a Kenyan user in Kenya.**
   - "KCSE results 2026": the KNEC official portal or SMS route is the best result.
   - "Gor Mahia vs AFC Leopards": the latest score or fixture, not club history.
   - "Safaricom customer care": the official number on safaricom.co.ke.
   - "Huduma Namba", "SHA registration" or "KRA PIN": official government pages first; a blog that copies them rates lower.
5. **Query interpretation.** Separate dominant, common and minor meanings ("Jaguar": the musician in Kenya, the car, or the animal). Results for the dominant local meaning rate highest.
6. **Flags.** Know when to use "Did not load", "Foreign language", "Porn" and "Upsetting/Offensive" instead of rating normally.
7. **Location and freshness.** If the query implies "near me" or "latest", old or far-away results fail even if well-written.
8. **Take notes per section** while reading, so the open-book exam is fast to search.
9. **Do the exam in one quiet sitting per part**, on a computer with stable internet.

## Risks

- Failing the exam. Usually you can only retry after a waiting period, so prepare properly the first time.
- Work volume varies. Some weeks have few tasks.
- Quality audits are continuous. Low scores mean removal.
- Exclusivity (above).

## Interview prep

There is no interview. The exam and ID check replace it.

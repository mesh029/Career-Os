## Watch-outs

- **Voice recording is required.** Video only if the portal shows a step. Scripts are in the portal pack. No colons when you speak.
- **Do the technical check at 4 PM Kenya time,** not in the morning. That is when this shift starts.
- **CCNA.** Confirm exam versus NetAcad before a form asks for the certificate ID.
- **Rate.** Only enter a number if the form forces one. Do not lowball a 40-hour evening shift.
- **Where you heard about BruntWork.** Use the same true answer as the other applications.
- **Fees.** Never pay. Report payment requests to applications@bruntwork.co.

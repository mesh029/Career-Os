## 4. Paste-ready answers

### How many years of IT Help Desk or Service Desk experience do you have, and have you provided remote support using a ticketing system?

Three years. At PATH I have run first- and second-line support for more than 120 users in Freshdesk and Zoho Desk, triaged by impact and SLA. Response time improved by about 25%. A large part of it is remote. Field staff call or message when they cannot sign in, and I walk them through mail, Teams, VPN and the device, then write the steps in the ticket.

### What hands-on experience do you have supporting Microsoft 365, Entra ID, Teams, Outlook, OneDrive, SharePoint, and Windows 10/11?

This is my daily support. I administer Microsoft 365 and Entra ID alongside Active Directory. I reset passwords, fix MFA, and sort Outlook, Teams, OneDrive and SharePoint access, including licences and security groups. On the device side I support Windows 10 and 11 remotely: sign-in, Office, printers and VPN. Uptime on these systems sits around 98%.

### Can you describe your experience managing employee accounts, assigning licenses, configuring access permissions, and deactivating accounts during offboarding?

I agree the access with the manager, then create the account in Entra ID, assign the Microsoft 365 licence, and add the groups for Teams, SharePoint, OneDrive and mail. I check the person can sign in, including MFA, before I close the ticket. When someone leaves, I disable the account the same day and remove the groups and the licence. I also review licences in between, removing inactive accounts and reassigning spare ones.

### What makes you the right person for this role?

For three years I have been the Microsoft 365 service desk for more than 120 users at PATH. I triage the ticket, fix Outlook, Teams, SharePoint, passwords and MFA, and I write down the cause. Starters get the licence and the access their role needs. Leavers lose it the same day. A lot of the users are at sites, not at a desk, so the support is already remote. I can work Monday to Friday on Sunnyside, Manitoba hours.

### Onboarding and offboarding

I confirm the role with the manager, then create the account, assign the Microsoft 365 licence, and add the Teams, SharePoint, OneDrive, mail and VPN access that role needs. I check they can sign in, including MFA, before I close the ticket. When someone leaves, I disable the account the same day, remove the groups and the licence, and note what was shared so it is not left open.

### A user cannot open Teams, mail or the VPN

I start with the account. Is it disabled, is the password expired, is MFA failing on the authenticator app? Then the device. Then the network, because a VPN failure after a good password is usually the connection, not the mailbox. I try the remote fix first, walk them through one step at a time, and write each step in the ticket. If the whole site is down, that ticket goes ahead of a single printer.

### Construction experience

My users are spread across more than 50 facilities. They are not at a desk. They need an account, Teams, mail and a connection before the day starts, and they call when it fails. That is the service desk a construction company needs for crews on sites. I have done it for three years, by phone and in person.

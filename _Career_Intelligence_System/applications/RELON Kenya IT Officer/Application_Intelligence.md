## Watch-outs

- **Reference number: RE/KE/AS/IT-/012.** Taken from the IT Officer section heading in RELON's official Angaza TOR (relonkenya.or.ke); MyJobMag leaves it out. Subject line: "Information Technology (Head Office-Nairobi) – RE/KE/AS/IT-/012".
- **Angaza Program.** The role sits under Angaza, a four-year consortium programme for economic inclusion of refugee and host-community young women aged 18–35, including persons with disabilities. Your DREAMS (adolescent girls and young women) field ICT work is the closest link; the cover letter makes it.
- **Terms.** One-year fixed term with a mandatory 3-month probation, subject to funding. Reports to the Administration Assistant, so you would likely be the only IT person.
- **Competencies listed in the full advert** include database management and ICT planning and user training. SQL databases are now on the CV.
- **Canvassing disqualifies.** Do not call or message RELON staff about the role.
- **Contract.** Ask about renewal after the first year, salary (gross or net) and whether it is full time in the Nairobi office.
- **Notice period.** Know your real PATH notice before the interview.
- **CCNA.** Listed as in the BRAC and other packs. Confirm whether it is the Cisco exam certification or the NetAcad course before the interview, and be ready to say which.
- **Fees.** Never pay for notarisation, certificates or assessments.

# Network Engineer — Co-operative Bank of Kenya

*Captured 2026-09-15 from co-opbank.co.ke careers. Deadline **30 September 2026**. Nairobi. Ref **NE/IID/2026**.*

**Apply by email:** application letter + detailed CV to **jobs@co-opbank.co.ke**, quoting reference **NE/IID/2026**.

Reports to **Head — Network & Infrastructure Support**.

## Role

Development, implementation, maintenance and administration of the **corporate data network**. Network and disaster recovery for uptime and business continuity. Documents all ICT network components and manages the entire inventory. Analyses network security needs and assists in developing standards, guidelines and statements of direction for corporate ICT system architectures.

- Manage corporate network performance against **business SLAs**
- Monitor network performance including **SNMP**, logs, events, general systems health; proactive remedial support
- Develop and maintain device access and control policies, data transmission, storage, business continuity, backup and security policies, monitoring profiles and notification rules to **compliance best practice**
- Plan and implement product upgrades, **firewall signature updates** and patch releases for all ICT assets
- Research external technical trends, evolving industry standards, and their application to business systems
- Define and document **architecture components** — hardware platform, operating system, execution, development, operation, distribution
- Install, support and maintain network devices; attend to system failures; resolve or coordinate resolution

## Requirements (verbatim weight)

- Bachelor's in Electrical & Electronics Engineering, Computer Science or IT-related field, **with possession of Cisco Certified Network Professional (CCNP) — "is a MUST"**. **CCIE preferred**
- Minimum **3 years** in planning, systems analysis, design, support and development of **complex LAN/WAN infrastructure**, plus project management experience
- Systems integration in multi-user, multi-platform environments: **UNIX (AIX, Solaris), Linux (RHEL, OpenShift), Windows**
- TCP/IP, **IP Telephony**, firewall appliances
- Network Engineering specialist with project management / consulting skills
- **Leadership** — lead mission-critical projects, lead multi-disciplinary teams
- Track record in **large, complex WANs, LANs and client/server systems**
- Modern information security threats and effective solutions
- **Multivendor: Cisco, Checkpoint, Avaya**
- **Extensive knowledge of Service Provider networks**, SP equipment and protocols
- Project management in cross-functional environments, multiple concurrent projects

## What this role actually is

A **bank core-network engineer** seat: enterprise WAN, service-provider links, Checkpoint firewalls, Avaya telephony, routing at scale, under a Head of Network & Infrastructure Support. It is one full level above multi-site LAN/WAN support, and Co-op Bank is a high-volume employer that filters on stated certifications first.

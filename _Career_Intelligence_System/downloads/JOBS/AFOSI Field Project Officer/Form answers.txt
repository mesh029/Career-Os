1) Most relevant digital / technology-enabled development project

Project: County Health Systems Operations Platform (NDWH & CBS) — built while supporting PATH digital health work across Kakamega, Vihiga, Nyamira and Kisumu.

Objectives: Give program and ICT teams one place to track facility reporting status (NDWH vs CBS), EMR deployment, IT assets and support incidents so gaps are visible early and reporting stays on time.

My responsibilities: Product ownership end to end — gather field/program requirements, design the data model and workflows, build and test the platform, train users on the tool, and use it to spot missing facilities and open tickets. Day to day I also supported KenyaEMR/DHIS2 and PATH project data workflows (including PRISM alongside DHIS2), with SQL/Excel validation and dashboards.

Technologies: Next.js, TypeScript, Prisma/MySQL (cloud), REST APIs, dashboards/maps; operational stack included DHIS2, KenyaEMR, Excel/SQL, Power BI/Grafana, Microsoft 365.

Results: A working multi-county ops platform with 40+ APIs and modules for reporting, EMR overview, assets and tickets; faster visibility on missing facility reports; ~40% less manual reporting grind through validation/automation; digital skills transfer to 80+ staff so adoption stuck.

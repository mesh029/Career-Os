## PHASE 7 — Career Growth Advisor: where the market is going

**Digital-health trends favoring you (lean in):**
- **Interoperability & FHIR** — becoming mandatory; still scarce talent in the region → learn it now (Tier 1).
- **National systems (Kenya):** TaifaCare/SHA/UHC digitization → sustained demand for EMR/DHIS2 people.
- **Data-for-decisions / surveillance analytics** — post-COVID, funders prioritize real-time data → your dashboards matter.
- **AI in health ops** — clinical documentation, triage, data-quality automation → pair your Python/AI-tool skills with health data.
- **Digital Public Goods (DPGs)** — OpenMRS, DHIS2, OpenHIE → global, remote-friendly, community-driven careers.

**Broader trends to ride:**
- **Remote/global health-data contracting** (USD income) once FHIR + a portfolio exist.
- **Cloud + health data** (secure health data on Azure/AWS) — a premium niche.
- **Platform/implementation consulting** — highest-leverage income for your profile in 12–24 months.

**Where to pivot before the market shifts:**
- From "IT support" → **health informatics specialist / analyst** (higher pay, more defensible). *(Primary move.)*
- From "generalist" → **interoperability/FHIR + analytics** (scarce, remote, global).
- Keep enterprise IT ops only as a paid floor while you climb the health-informatics ladder.

**Roles/titles that are growing (target these titles in searches):** Health Informatics Specialist/
Analyst, Digital Health Specialist, EMR/DHIS2 Implementation Officer, Interoperability Engineer,
Health Data Analyst, M&E/Data Officer (health), Solutions/Implementation Consultant (health systems).

## PHASE 8 — Continuous Coaching (the honest mirror)

### What's strong (build on it)
- A rare, valuable niche (health IT + EMR/DHIS2 + data) with real, recent, hands-on experience.
- Solid certification base and clear upward progression at a credible org.
- You already do senior-flavored work (training, dashboards, integrations, data quality).

### Where you're holding yourself back
1. **You dilute your identity.** 7+ personas across applications. Pick one, commit, compound. (See `03`.)
2. **You undersell on the channel that matters most (LinkedIn) and oversell on some CVs.** Both hurt credibility.
3. **Junior language.** "Supported," "assisted," "helped" dominate. Upgrade to ownership verbs:
   *led, built, designed, automated, delivered, improved, reduced, sustained.*
4. **Under-quantified.** You have great numbers (98% uptime, 120+ users, 40%/25%) but scatter them.
5. **Facts drift** (titles/dates). One source of truth fixes this permanently.
6. **Aiming below your ceiling** (L1 voice, customer support) — respect your own level.

### Language upgrades (junior → senior)
| Junior | Senior |
|---|---|
| "Helped support EMR systems" | "Sustained >98% uptime for KenyaEMR/DHIS2 across [N] facilities" |
| "Assisted with data" | "Built SQL/DQA validation that cut reporting time ~40%" |
| "Did troubleshooting" | "Led root-cause analysis reducing repeat incidents" |
| "Trained users" | "Built capacity of 80+ health workers, improving data accuracy/adoption" |
| "Worked with teams" | "Partnered with county/national/partner stakeholders to deliver [outcome]" |

### Confidence-building routine (before interviews)
- Keep a **"wins file"**: 8–10 STAR stories (Situation-Task-Action-Result), each with a number.
- Rehearse the **2-minute "tell me about yourself"** built on the one-line positioning.
- Prepare **3 stories** you can tell for almost any question: an uptime/incident win, a data-quality win, a training/stakeholder win.
- Do one **mock interview** per target role; record yourself; cut filler words.

### 30-60-90 coaching plan
- **30 days:** Lock facts; fix employer name; rewrite LinkedIn; publish 3 posts; start DHIS2 Academy + ITIL; apply to 5 health-informatics roles with the primary resume.
- **60 days:** FHIR fundamentals + 1 GitHub repo; publish a dashboard case study; 2 recommendations; weekly posting; 10 targeted applications.
- **90 days:** PL-300 (or Google Data Analytics) done; OpenMRS-on-Docker project; portfolio case studies live; measure KPI deltas (profile views, DMs, interviews) and re-plan.

### Recommended inputs
- **Books:** *The Tech Resume Inside Out* (Orosz); *On Writing Well* (Zinsser); *Never Split the Difference* (Voss) for negotiation.
- **Practice:** STAR/behavioral drills; salary-negotiation scripts before offers.
- **Communities:** OpenMRS Talk, DHIS2 Community, HELINA, Kenya digital-health groups.

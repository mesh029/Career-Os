## B. The dominant pattern (the single biggest problem)

**You are applying as a different person every time.** Across 17 applications you present at
least **seven different professional identities** — health informatics specialist, cybersecurity
analyst, sysadmin, CRM/SaaS consultant, web/Webflow developer, customer-support agent, talent-
acquisition/ops associate.

Consequences:
- **No compounding brand.** Each application starts from zero; nothing reinforces the next.
- **Facts drift.** Titles and dates differ between versions (see `01_Career_Profile.md §7.2`), a
  recruiter cross-check risk.
- **You often aim below your ceiling** (L1 voice helpdesk, customer support) *and* occasionally
  into domains where you're underqualified (MEL Officer at a wildlife conservancy, Webflow dev).
- **Effort is spread thin**, lowering quality per application and therefore interview rate.

**Diagnosis:** classic "spray and tailor" from a place of uncertainty. The fix is not more
tailoring — it's a **primary identity** with at most 2–3 disciplined variants.
